Hi, I'm Sakina.

I build small tools, mostly local AI stuff and CLIs for sites that never bothered with an API. [rummage](https://github.com/soffits/rummage) is the one I use most. It searches my notes and old chats by meaning, on a plain CPU. I also make the odd Minecraft mod, like [OpenPlayer](https://github.com/soffits/OpenPlayer), which puts AI companions into the game as player-shaped NPCs.

I don't fully trust leaderboards. [ECI Live](https://github.com/soffits/eci-live) started because Epoch's published capabilities index barely moved while the data under it changed every day, so now I refit it myself every day. Lately I've been watching AI agents play Portal 2 and trying to work out how much of a score is the model and how much is everything around it.

Someday I want to make a small world that writes its own history, with villagers who pass what they know on to each other and sometimes lose it along the way. I've read far too much about how knowledge spreads and gets lost not to try it.

And if you ever want to lose me for an afternoon, mention the Voynich manuscript.

I write about bugs and the things I build on [my blog](https://soffits.github.io/).
